Frequently Asked Questions
How much does it cost to submit an idea? ▼
Submitting ideas is completely free. IdeaHub charges a small commission only when your idea is sold.
Can I submit a patented idea? ▼
Yes! You can submit patented ideas and upload your patent certificate. Our admin team will verify it and add a Verified Patent badge to your listing, increasing buyer trust.
What happens after I submit a proposal for an investor request? ▼
The investor reviews all proposals and can shortlist, invite to pitch, or select yours. You'll get notified at every stage. If selected, escrow is locked and you'll need to deliver the idea.
How does the escrow system protect me? ▼
When an investor selects your proposal, they lock the agreed payment in escrow before you deliver anything. This guarantees you get paid once you deliver the work.

Frequently Asked Questions
What types of problems can I post? ▼
Any business problem, innovation challenge, or market gap you need solved. From tech solutions to marketing strategies, product concepts to business models.
Is my request visible to everyone? ▼
Yes, requests are public so creators can find and respond to them. However, you control which proposals you engage with and can keep communications private.
What if I'm not happy with the delivered idea? ▼
You can raise a dispute before releasing payment. Our support team will review the case and mediate between you and the creator.

Frequently Asked Questions
What expansion types can I offer? ▼
You can list your business for Franchise, License, Joint Venture, Distribution, or White Label partnerships. You can offer multiple types on the same listing.
Does IdeaHub handle contracts or payments for business deals? ▼
No — business expansion deals are negotiated and finalized directly between the parties outside the platform. IdeaHub provides the discovery and connection platform. Legal contracts are handled independently.
Can I restrict which countries can apply? ▼
Yes. When listing your business you can specify territories you're open to, and filter applicants by country.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is a Virtual Manager on IdeaHub? ▼
A Virtual Manager helps idea creators manage their projects, coordinate tasks, handle communications and keep development on track — remotely.
Do I need to be a licensed attorney to join as Patent Attorney? ▼
Yes. You'll need to submit your licence number and bar association details for verification. Unverified profiles can still be listed but won't have the verified badge.
Can I restrict which countries I work with? ▼
Yes. In your support profile you can set your service type to "Specific Countries" (only serve listed countries) or "Restricted Countries" (exclude certain countries). Clients will see a warning before hiring.

Frequently Asked Questions
What do I get when I buy an idea? ▼
You receive the full idea documentation including the concept description, business plan details, research, technical specifications (if applicable) and any supporting files the creator provides.
Are ideas exclusive once purchased? ▼
Once an idea is sold, it's marked as sold and removed from the marketplace. The creator cannot sell it again to another buyer.
What if I'm not satisfied with the idea? ▼
Payment is held in escrow and only released when you confirm satisfaction. If there's an issue, you can raise a dispute before confirming delivery.
