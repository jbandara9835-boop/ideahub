  3. The Buyer confirms receipt and satisfaction.
  4. Funds are released to the Creator upon confirmation.

If the Buyer neither confirms nor reports a problem within 7 days of delivery, the funds are released to the Creator automatically. The same 7-day rule applies to Idea Requests and to hired professionals.

11. Virtual Managers and Mandates

A Virtual Manager acts for one client under a written mandate agreed on Picela. The mandate is between the client and the manager; Picela hosts the payments and the records but is not a party to it.

  • One side only. A manager may not act for both sides of a deal, or for two competing clients in the same industry at the same time. Picela enforces this automatically and may end a mandate that breaks it.
  • Client approval. Anything binding (a purchase, a proposal, an application, hiring someone) happens only when the client approves it on Picela, and the client pays through Picela. A manager never receives the client's money other than the fees in the mandate.
  • Fees. Retainers are paid for each 30-day period and released to the manager at the end of it unless the client reports a problem. Hourly fees are paid when the client approves the timesheet. A success fee applies to deals completed through Picela that the manager prepared or recommended during the mandate, or within 90 days after it ends for deals introduced during it. Milestone fees for managed ventures are held until the client approves the milestone. Picela adds its service fee to each payment.
  • Confidentiality. A manager must keep everything learned about a client confidential, during and after the mandate, including anything learned in a hiring process (CV review, questions or video interview) even if they are not hired.
  • Problems. Either side can report a problem. Picela reviews the activity log, messages and files and decides whether held fees are released or refunded.
