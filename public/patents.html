How it works

Describe your invention

A guided form covers what the attorney needs: the problem, how it works, what's new, and drawings.

Get a fixed quote

Your chosen attorney reviews it and sends one price, what it includes, and when they'll file.

Pay into escrow

Accept the quote and pay. IdeaHub holds the money while the attorney prepares and files.

Filed, then released

The attorney uploads the official filing receipt. You confirm and the fee is released.

🔒 ConfidentialOnly the attorney you choose and IdeaHub admins can read your invention. Files are stored privately, never in public links.
🛡️ Protected paymentIf the attorney doesn't file, open a dispute and IdeaHub reviews it. Refunds come from escrow.
✅ Verified attorneysOnly patent attorneys whose identity and credentials IdeaHub has checked can receive applications.
